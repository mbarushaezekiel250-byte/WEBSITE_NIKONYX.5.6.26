Help Center

Find answers to common questions about Nikonyx.

Start by completing the style questionnaire on first launch — this sets your style profile. Then add at least 5 garments to your wardrobe by photographing them. Once your wardrobe has items, tap "Create Outfits" on the home screen and tell NX the occasion (date, work, casual). The AI will suggest 3 complete outfits using your clothes.

The 3D avatar pipeline requires the Ready Player Me API credentials to be configured. If you see "2D Avatar Ready" it means the 3D API was unavailable — your selfie is still saved securely. To get a full 3D avatar, use the Body Scan feature (360° scan) from the home screen which uses a separate on-device pipeline.

Stand 2–2.5 metres from the phone in form-fitting clothes against a plain background. The app guides you through a checklist, then captures 52+ frames as you rotate slowly 360°. The scan takes about 25 seconds. Body landmarks are extracted to estimate your measurements (chest, waist, hips, inseam). All processing happens on-device for privacy.

Navigate to the Try-On tab. Upload a full-body photo of yourself (or use your saved body photo). Browse your wardrobe or saved outfits, select a garment, and tap "Try On". The AI composites the selected item onto your photo. You can save results, compare options, and share directly to Friends Space.

The AI works best with at least 10–15 wardrobe items across different categories (tops, bottoms, outerwear, shoes). With fewer items it has limited combinations to choose from. Also ensure your style profile questionnaire is complete — the AI uses your stated preferences, favourite colours, and fit preference to personalise suggestions.

Processed measurement data (numbers only, no photos) is stored securely in your account and deleted within 24 hours unless you save your avatar. Your scan data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Tap the Wardrobe tab then the + button. You can take a photo of a garment or pick from your camera roll. AI will auto-detect: category (top, bottom, shoes, outerwear, accessories), colour, style tag, and season. Review and confirm or edit the tags, then save. The background is automatically removed for clean display.

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your wardrobe data, saved outfits, body scan data, and avatar files will be permanently deleted within 48 hours. This action cannot be undone. If you need help, contact us via the Contact page before proceeding.
