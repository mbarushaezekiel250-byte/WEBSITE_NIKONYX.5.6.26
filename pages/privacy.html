Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026  ·  Nikonyx / Nikonyxlook

📜
Overview

Nikonyx ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it. We built Nikonyx with a privacy-first approach — your body data stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to save it.

📊
Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the Nikonyx service:

  • Account information: name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Style profile: preferences, favourite colours, fit preferences from questionnaire
  • Wardrobe data: clothing photos, AI-generated tags (category, colour, style, season)
  • Outfit history: saved outfits and calendar outfit plans
  • App usage data: anonymised analytics to improve features

We do not collect advertising identifiers, sell your data, or track you across other apps.

👤
Body Scan & Avatar Privacy

The 3D body scan is processed with your privacy as the top priority:

  • Body segmentation and pose estimation run on-device
  • Raw body photos are never sent to external servers without your explicit consent
  • Only processed measurement data (numbers — not photos) is stored if you save your avatar
  • Scan data is automatically deleted within 24 hours unless you save your avatar
  • You can delete your avatar and all associated data at any time from Settings
🔒 Your body photos never leave your device during the scan process. Only if you tap "Save Avatar" do any processed files get stored to our secure cloud.

⚙
How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Nikonyx service:

  • Generating personalised AI outfit suggestions via your wardrobe and style profile
  • Powering the virtual try-on feature with your saved body photo
  • Connecting your wardrobe to calendar events for outfit planning
  • Improving AI accuracy through anonymised, aggregated usage signals

We never use your data for advertising, profiling for third parties, or purposes outside the Nikonyx app.

👥
Third-Party Services

Nikonyx uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase / Firestore (Google) — authentication, database, file storage
  • Anthropic Claude API — AI outfit generation and styling advice
  • Supabase — wardrobe image storage
  • Remove.bg — clothing background removal
  • Ready Player Me — optional 3D avatar generation (only if you use this feature)

We share only the minimum data required for each service to function. We do not allow third parties to use your data for their own purposes.

🔒
Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data at any time via Settings → Edit Profile
  • Delete your account and all associated data permanently
  • Export your wardrobe data in a portable format (contact us to request)
  • Withdraw consent for any optional data processing
