Platform Features

Every Feature You Need

Nikonyx combines AI styling, 3D scanning, virtual try-on, and social fashion — all in one app.

🤖

AI Outfit Suggestions

A multi-agent AI system powered by Claude recommends complete outfits for any occasion, pulling only from items in your own wardrobe.

  • Occasion-aware: date, wedding, work, casual, interview
  • Weather and location context for smart seasonal picks
  • Explains why each combination works together
  • Prevents repeating looks tracked in your calendar
👔

Wardrobe Manager

Your digital closet. Photograph each garment and AI auto-tags every item instantly — no manual entry needed.

  • Auto-detects: category, colour, style tag, season
  • Background removal for clean clothing display
  • Filter and search by any tag or category
  • Synced to cloud — access from any device
🪪

Virtual Try-On Room

See exactly how any outfit looks on your body before committing — using AI image compositing technology.

  • Upload a full-body photo once, try unlimited outfits
  • Realistic drape and fit simulation
  • Save and compare try-on results side by side
  • Share to Friends Space or Style Feed directly
⸻

3D Avatar Creation

Rotate 360° in front of your camera. Nikonyx builds a precise digital twin of your body for accurate size recommendations.

  • 52-frame 360° capture pipeline
  • Extracts shoulder, chest, waist, hip, inseam measurements
  • Exports as GLB file — wear outfits in 3D viewer
  • Connects to virtual try-on and size recommendation engine
👥

Friends Space & Style Feed

A social fashion layer built into the app. Share your best looks, get inspired by friends, and build your personal style identity in public.

  • Post outfit photos with occasion and style tags
  • Like, comment, and follow friends with similar taste
  • Discover trending looks from the community
  • Share AI-generated outfits directly from the result screen
📅

Calendar Outfit Planning

Plan what you'll wear to every event in advance. AI connects your wardrobe to your schedule.

  • Add fashion events with occasion and weather context
  • AI pre-selects the best outfit for each date
  • Tracks outfit history — no outfit repeats
  • Week, month, and day calendar views
🛒

Smart Shopping

When your wardrobe is missing a key piece, NX finds it for you — without pushing random ads.

  • Only triggers when a wardrobe gap is detected
  • Searches trusted retailers ranked by style match
  • Links directly to product pages for purchase
  • Never suggests items your wardrobe already covers
